SolePro is the app for people who work for themselves: a massage therapist, a barber, a tattoo artist, a hairdresser, a tennis coach, a landscaper, a pool tech, a cleaner. You're the whole business. SolePro handles the office.

Everything is set up with your own words, services, prices, hours, forms, logo and colors, so it feels like your business, not someone else's software.

TODAY
- Who's next, and what to know before they walk in: their notes, health flags, what you did last time
- One short to-do list: who hasn't paid, notes to write, forms missing, papers coming up for renewal
- Tomorrow's confirmation texts and yesterday's thank-yous, written for you

SCHEDULE
- Day, week and month, with your hours, breaks, days off and vacations
- Book, move or cancel in a few taps, with Undo
- Book again on a calendar that shows when a client usually comes back
- Holidays marked, and the weather where you work

CLIENTS
- Everyone A to Z, with search and filters: coming up, due back, new, health notes, needs a form
- Each client's page: every visit, notes, forms and what they've spent
- Visit notes in seconds, including SOAP notes for massage, with tap-to-add phrases and "Same as last time"
- Bring in your client list from another app, a spreadsheet saved as CSV, or your phone's contacts

FORMS AND PAPERS
- Intake forms and waivers your clients fill in and sign on a phone
- Text a client a link to their form
- Print or save any form or record as a PDF
- Keep your license, insurance and certificates on file, with a reminder before they expire

MONEY
- Today, this week and this year at a glance, and where you're headed
- Every month against last year
- Payments and costs, with a photo of the receipt

MADE TO BE DEPENDABLE
- Your book is on your phone, so it opens with no signal and saves your changes when you're back
- Backed up every night
- The same book on your phone, tablet and computer
- Light and dark
